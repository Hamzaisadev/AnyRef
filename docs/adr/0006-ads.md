# ADR 0006: Ad Monetization, Consent Framework, and Approval Strategy

- **Status**: Accepted
- **Date**: 2026-10-29
- **Author**: Platform Architecture Team
- **Context**: Anyref's free tier is monetized through advertisements to offset AI model costs, paired with a $5/month Plus tier that eliminates ads. Display networks (Google AdSense, Prebid, Carbon) enforce strict site eligibility, user-generated content (UGC) safety, and EEA/UK GDPR consent rules.

---

## 1. Ad Network Approval Policies & Research

1. **The "Walled Garden" Problem**:
   - Google AdSense crawlers cannot index pages behind authentication (`/library`, `/workspace`, `/entries`). Submitting an application with only authenticated routes results in immediate rejection: *"Site down or unavailable"* or *"Valuable inventory: No content"*.
2. **Public Content Strategy**:
   - To achieve network approval and maintain high inventory quality, Anyref hosts public, crawlable, indexable educational content:
     - Public landing page (`/`)
     - In-depth guide: "AI Poster Prompt Engineering & Deconstruction" (`/guides/poster-prompts`)
     - Public Curated Showcase (`/showcase`): owner-created reference templates demonstrating visual design principles.
   - **Zero User UGC Exposure**: User-uploaded images, brand assets, and generated prompts are strictly private (RLS enforced) and never indexed by search engines or monetized directly.
3. **Placements and User Experience**:
   - Allowed: Library bottom banner, sidebar slot, and generating wait screen.
   - Prohibited: Login, Admin routes, Billing, Legal pages, form fields, and direct prompt copy actions.
   - CLS Protection: All `<AdSlot>` containers use reserved, static CSS heights (e.g. `min-h-[250px]` or `min-h-[90px]`) guaranteeing Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS) < 0.1.

---

## 2. Consent & Privacy (EEA/UK GDPR & CCPA)

1. **TCF 2.2 / CMP Standards**:
   - For visitors from EEA/UK, personalized ads require explicit, affirmative opt-in before ad scripts execute.
   - If consent is refused or deferred, fallback to contextual, non-personalized ads or empty slots.
2. **Audit Logging & Records**:
   - Consent choices (`kind in ('tos', 'privacy', 'ads_personalized', 'ads_basic')`) are recorded in the `consent_records` database table.
3. **Staff and Plus Exemption**:
   - Platform staff (`owner`, `admin`) and `plus` subscribers resolve to `showAds = false`. Ad components return `null` immediately and never load third-party ad scripts.

---

## 3. Decision

- Implement pluggable `<AdSlot>` component evaluating `showAds`, global switch, and user consent.
- Enforce strict route exclusions and reserved dimensions.
- Ship public indexable guide and showcase pages to satisfy ad network review requirements.
